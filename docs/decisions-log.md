## 2026-11-12: Method B: the random number tool is sealed before the pick (draft, #12)

- **The tool is required at publish**, not only before "Close entries", so a backstop close at `closes_at` still seals one. It can change until the close (Draws page, on the audit chain), then it is sealed with the entry fingerprint. "Close entries" is disabled until it's named.
- **The livestream link is required** to record the result (https only).
- **Certificate and /verify** carry the plain statement: "Verity verified the sealed entry list and that the winning ticket was on it. The random selection was made externally on the linked livestream." The in-browser verifier now runs for external draws as well.
